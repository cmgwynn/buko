Who we are

Buko is operated by [OWNER OR COMPANY NAME], [COUNTRY / STATE]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account: email address and sign-in details.
  • Profile and goals: goal, sex, birthday, height, weight, activity level, units and dietary preferences, used to calculate your targets.
  • What you log: food diary, meal plans, recipes, grocery lists, workouts, body measurements, progress photos and planning preferences such as foods or exercises to skip.
  • Health-app data (optional): if you connect Apple Health or Health Connect, the activity, workouts and weight you allow.
  • Feedback you send: your message, its category, the screen it came from, platform and app version.
  • Anonymous usage counts (you can turn this off): each day you open Buko, a total for that day, platform and sign-up week goes up by one. No account ID, device ID or content is stored with it.
  • Optional crash signals: off by default. If you turn them on, Buko sends only a fixed error category, platform and app release.

How we use it

To run the app: calculate targets, generate meal and training plans, sync your data between devices, answer support requests and decide what to improve. We do not sell your personal data. We do not use health or fitness data for advertising or ad targeting.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and server functions.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central receive barcode and food search queries, without your account details.
  • Anthropic (only if you turn on Coach or AI recipe features) receives the minimum context needed for a response. Names, email and birthday are excluded.
  • Instacart (only when you tap Order on Instacart) receives the names, quantity notes and barcodes of your unchecked grocery items. It does not receive your account details or health data. Buko may earn a commission on orders.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google process Buko Plus purchases using a pseudonymous ID.
  • No advertising: Buko does not show ads or use advertising SDKs.

Your choices and rights

  • Export your data or delete your account from Profile → Account & data in the app, or on the web at delete account.
  • Turn usage counts, crash signals, Coach and health connections on or off under Profile → Privacy.
  • Deleting your account removes your Buko data. It does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, and it does not delete records stored in Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Retention and security

Your data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account, except where the law requires us to keep it. Database rows are protected by owner-only access rules, and connections are encrypted. [Describe the backup retention period.]
